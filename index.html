How to play

Every level gives you three numbers. Build the four equations that connect them. Level 1 uses addition and subtraction; Level 2 uses multiplication and division; Level 3 asks you to identify the family first.

How to play

Build a fact family from the three numbers you are given.

1
Look at the three numbers.
They are related and can be used to make a family of equations.
2
Build each equation.
Tap number and operation tiles to fill each line. Most families have four distinct equations; square-number multiplication families have two.

Game settings

Adjust the game for your learners.

Starting-number range
Both starting numbers are always whole numbers from 1 to 12. Answers may be larger than 12; for example, 12 × 12 = 144. Students cannot select or change the range.
Feedback style
Immediate feedback checks each selected row as you answer, or check all rows together at the end.
Hints
Hints are optional and never affect mastery accuracy.
Mastery requirement
Each level requires at least 10 completed fact families and 90% accuracy across family-identification and equation attempts.
Fixed for this first version to keep progression consistent.
Unlock all levels
Use this if a student has already completed the game but their saved browser progress was lost after clearing cookies or cache. This unlocks all three levels without requiring them to repeat the mastery process.

This does not erase progress or change the student's accuracy statistics.
